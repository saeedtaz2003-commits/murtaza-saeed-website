# Biz Buzz desktop commentary standard — 15 October 2026

Preserve the square meme, controls, order logic, destinations and mobile sticky layout.
On desktop, fit commentary and utilities beside the image in its fixed-height panel.
Keep the bottom Get Updates / Explore More Memes group aligned with the image frame.
Prefer a complete one-line headline where practical; never truncate or clip it.
Write concise, self-contained, accurate commentary with natural subject context, normally
80–120 words or shorter when required by available space. Preserve suspense where approved.
Edit copy before reducing comfortable reading sizes. Do not create internal scrollbars.
Check every slide for overflow and overlaps at desktop and 320/375/430px mobile widths.
Keep archive thumbnails compact and preserve their distinct 25–40 word summaries.
Withdrawn items retain their source data and image for future redesign.
